I am currently a postdoctoral research fellow in Statistics at the [University of Michigan](https://www.umich.edu/). I completed my doctoral studies at [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/) in 2026. Prior to that, I received my B.S. degree in Statistics from [the School of Mathematical Sciences](http://www.math.zju.edu.cn/mathen/), Zhejiang University, in 2021. I was a visiting scholar at [the Wharton School](https://www.wharton.upenn.edu/) from 2024 to 2025.

My research interests include large-scale hypothesis testing, conformal inference, selective inference, and empirical Bayes methods.
